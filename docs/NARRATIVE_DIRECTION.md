# Active narrative direction: steampunk meets Wilkie Collins

Date: 2026-10-06.
Status: user-confirmed direction and scope, not a completed new world or approved replacement cast.

Read this before WORLD_AND_CAST.md or BELLWETHER_REBUILD.md. The latest conversation supersedes the earlier routing that made Bellwether the presumptive next setting.

## What the user confirmed

The user requested a clean-sheet rebuild of the fiction rather than repairs to the first cast. After the Bellwether proposal, the user observed that the first version had more of the intended steampunk-meets-Wilkie-Collins feel. The user then agreed with the analysis that the rebuild had overcorrected toward restraint, institutional debate and archival custody, losing theatricality, menace, eccentricity and domestic intrigue.

Keep the stronger causal discipline. Recover the original genre ambition. Do not confuse less extravagant fiction with better-founded fiction.

The user approved that direction, not a third set of names, a new crime, a final title, or the automatic reinstatement of the Vaudrey cast.

## The game is still the same game

The planned full game has 21 cards: six suspects, six methods and nine locations. One card from each category is hidden. Eighteen cards are dealt to three investigators, six each. Suggestions, private refutations, notebook review and a final accusation remain the deduction structure.

The proposed Bellwether concealment-object category was not approved. Do not implement containers in place of methods merely because they are easier to make mutually exclusive. Preserve the methods category unless the user separately approves a change.

The existing small tutorial is not already the full 21-card game. Preserve it. This direction does not authorize changing code, saved games, deployment, opponent policies, interface pacing or the rules engine.

The fiction supplies identity, atmosphere, motives, dialogue and meaningful places. It does not add a fourth hidden question, a mandatory psychological puzzle, or a separately authored clue sequence for every deal. Historical interpretation is not an additional victory condition.

## Creative brief

Create a peculiar, elaborate domestic world that would be interesting to enter before the case begins. The household should contain pleasures, habits, rivalries, affection, vanity, appetites and ordinary obligations as well as secrets.

Machinery is in present use. It delivers messages, summons people, registers defined events, supports household routines and creates opportunities for inconvenience and manipulation. It is neither ornamental brasswork nor merely an obsolete device that once produced the disputed record.

The installation must have intelligible limits. A mechanical record proves only what the apparatus can actually register, not intent, identity or omniscient truth. Dramatic machinery must not silently introduce case facts incompatible with the deal.

Character desires should be concrete: love, money, recognition, comfort, privacy, revenge, social standing, freedom from interference. Themes about knowledge and authority may emerge from those desires; the cast should not be assembled as spokespersons for ethical positions.

Make room for genuine charm, petty tyranny, offensive politeness, domestic comedy, erotic or romantic tension where appropriate, eccentric enthusiasms and menace. These are tonal possibilities, not a requirement that every person possess all of them. Different people must have different rhythms and social tactics. Do not make every speaker a polished aphorist.

An eccentricity need not be a symptom, clue or disguised admission. Kindness may be real even in a compromised person. Some relationships need no hidden leverage at all. Not everyone needs equivalent historical guilt, and not every pair needs a major secret.

The first draft's use of private papers, household authority, a physician or the continuing influence of a dead person was not inherently wrong. None is mandatory or prohibited in the fresh build. Select particulars for dramatic value and causal coherence, not to mechanically invert the old draft.

A historical catastrophe is not a prerequisite. Do not force every relationship to originate in one disaster or spend the opening explaining a comprehensive old scandal. Let the present household establish itself through behavior.

## Tone reference from the agreed discussion

The following is an illustrative register, not approved cast, plot, dialogue assets or a case clue:

At dinner, a pneumatic carrier strikes its receiving cup behind the hostess.

She reads the message, folds it twice, and resumes discussing the soup.

“Shall I disconnect the apparatus?” asks the engineer.

“Certainly not. It is the sender I object to.”

Her companion holds out a hand.

The hostess places the message beneath her plate.

The useful quality is the interaction of working apparatus, social comedy, intimacy and concealment without an explanatory speech about information. Do not promote this example into a fixed event that determines a suspect's opportunity or guilt.

## Standards for the next creative proposal

Develop a compact household premise and a small ensemble scene alongside the new cast, rather than locking another extensive biography document before the characters work together. A dinner is one possible scene test, not an approved mandatory opening. Demonstrate distinct voices and conflicting wants through actions.

Privately test custody, chronology, knowledge sources and the actual meanings of method and location before presenting them as settled facts. Preserve the distinction between authorial history, a character's testimony, belief and guaranteed case evidence.

The independent suspect/method/location categories imply 324 full-game combinations. Proposed methods must be genuinely distinguishable alternatives with plausible access for each suspect and compatible locations; replacing the method category is not a substitute for doing that design work. Do not claim combinations are validated until they have been checked.

A scene is a characterization test, not a change to how evidence is acquired. Personality and humor must not leak private cards or add untracked alibis, accusations, physical findings or other deductions that contradict the engine.

Keep apparatus puzzles, expanded interrogation systems and bulk voice/music/image production outside this step. Existing text-first, manually paced, VoiceOver-oriented interaction remains the baseline.
