11. During an ordinary end-turn, confirm both the ending investigator and the new investigator are read automatically in order. Check a forced human response, incorrect accusation and completion if practical. Try opening review during narration and stepping quickly: speech may be interrupted by your navigation, but repeat/history must retain the text, focus must remain useful and no extra game action may occur.
