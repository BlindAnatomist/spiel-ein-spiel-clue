Automatic exchanges should not repeatedly reintroduce Rowan or Rowan’s unchanged location.

Try opening review during narration and starting turns quickly: speech may be interrupted by your navigation, but repeat/history must retain the text, focus must remain useful and no extra game action may occur. There is no browser speech-completion signal or speech-duration timer.

Report the button or lesson title, what VoiceOver said, whether it completed the batch without swiping, any duplicate or missing sentences, where focus landed immediately and after settling, and what you expected.
