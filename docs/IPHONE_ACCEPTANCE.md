# iPhone VoiceOver acceptance: pending user testing

Automated tests are separate from this walkthrough. No one has claimed you tested or approved this interface.

1. Open the published preview on your iPhone with VoiceOver. Choose Learn to play. Confirm that you reach the first objective and can read its explanation without an automatic advance.
2. Choose Continue, then Review my starting evidence. Read Mira and Copied key and the explanation that held cards are excluded. Return to game. Confirm focus returns to the review control, and choose Continue lesson.
3. Move to Workshop. Continue to the suggestion. Open Make a suggestion; choose Mira and Copied key. Review, then submit. Confirm the location is Workshop and that selecting values never submits by itself.
4. Choose Next exchange. Read Lena's private evidence. Open the notebook, choose Locations, open Workshop, and check the source. Return and continue. Use Explain this step or Repeat last exchange whenever needed; neither should change the game.
5. Continue the guided exchanges until you answer Lena. Choose either matching card. Confirm you understand that only that card is shown to Lena. At the next question with no matching cards, confirm Cannot refute is the only response.
6. Follow the controlled Archive and Garden questions. Check that the tutorial waits for both passes before explaining the Garden deduction. Review older transcript entries without advancing play.
7. Complete the suspect and method questions. Open the final accusation, select the taught answer, review it, then cancel once. Confirm no accusation occurred and focus returned sensibly. Reopen, review and confirm to finish the case.
8. During any lesson, reload the page. Choose Learn to play and Resume saved case. Verify you return to the same confirmed lesson, with no repeated game action. Starting a replacement should require explicit confirmation.
9. Choose Play a game. Confirm the cards differ from the fixed practice as a fresh deal permits. Try rules help and notebook review. Check that a bot question requiring your answer pauses until you choose a legal response.
10. From the menu, choose Public observation and Listen to bots play. Advance several exchanges, repeat and review the transcript. Public mode should not offer private evidence or notebook. Start a separate followed-investigator case and confirm whose perspective is named.

Report the button or lesson title, what VoiceOver said, where focus landed, and what you expected. The most important acceptance question is whether the case teaches enough for you to choose actions independently, not just whether controls can be reached.
